To-Do List App

This is a simple task manager that runs entirely in your browser. It helps you keep track of daily tasks and your progress is automatically saved using Local Storage.

How to use

  • Type a task and press Enter or click Add
  • Click the Checkbox to mark a task as complete
  • Click the trash icon to delete a task
  • Use All / Active / Completed to filter your tasks
  • Click the🌙/☀️ iconto toggle Dark and Light mode
  • Your tasks are automatically saved in your browser
