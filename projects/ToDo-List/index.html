To-Do List App Pro

Manage your tasks like a pro. Add due dates, set priority, search, and edit tasks. All data saves to your browser automatically.

How to use

  • Type a task and press Enter or click Add
  • Click the Checkbox to mark a task as complete
  • Click the trash icon 🗑️ to delete a task
  • Use All / Active / Completed to filter your tasks
  • Click the🌙/☀️ icon to toggle Dark and Light mode
  • Your tasks are automatically saved in your browser
