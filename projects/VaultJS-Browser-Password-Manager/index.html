VaultJS

Your passwords. Encrypted in your browser.

VaultJS is a zero-knowledge password manager. Your master password never leaves your device. We only store encrypted gibberish on the server.

How it works:

  1. Register: Create account. We generate a unique salt for you.
  2. Login: We send your salt back. Your browser turns your password + salt into an encryption key.
  3. Vault: All passwords are encrypted/decrypted in your browser using AES-256-GCM before they touch the internet.

Important: If you forget your master password, we CANNOT recover it.
